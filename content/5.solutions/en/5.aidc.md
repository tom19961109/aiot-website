## AI Defect Detection System

The AI product line primarily focuses on smart defect detection (ADC/AiDC) and automated failure classification (FDC). 

![AiDC AI Defect Detection System](/images/ADC.png){.rounded-lg height="600" width="600"}

By collecting samples and training AI models, the system performs precise visual inspection and defect classification. After implementation, it resolves yield‑improvement bottlenecks, reduces labor requirements by over 80%, and increases the number of products inspected per day by tenfold.
