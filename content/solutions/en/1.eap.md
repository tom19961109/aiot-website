## Equipment Automation Program

It is the core system for equipment control and data management, serving as the bridge between the MES (Manufacturing Execution System) and production equipment. 

![EAP 設備自動化平台](/images/EAP.png)

It primarily communicates with equipment through the SECS/GEM protocol, enabling real‑time monitoring, production parameter and recipe dispatch, and automated data collection. Implementing EAP significantly reduces human operation errors, improves product yield, and forms a critical foundation for unmanned and smart manufacturing.
