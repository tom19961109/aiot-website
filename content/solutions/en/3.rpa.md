## Robotic Process Automation
A “virtual intelligent operation robot” tailor‑made for factory environments. 

::picture-and-text

### Feature Applications
By integrating with the RCM system, the RPA can automatically execute repetitive manual tasks—such as automatic equipment reset, parameter selection, and measurement—enabling network connectivity for non‑SECS equipment.  

### Technical Features
Built‑in AI vision processing engine supporting OCR, CV, and DL technologies, capable of recognizing graphics and text to extract values from equipment screens. 

### Implementation Benefits 
Significantly increases the man‑to‑machine ratio (e.g., from 1:7 to 1:75+), reduces human operation errors, and accelerates the resolution of abnormal issues.
