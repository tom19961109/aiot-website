## Remote Control Management System

Allowing engineers to leave the cleanroom while still maintaining real‑time visibility into equipment status.
Primarily extending the screen, keyboard, and mouse signals of cleanroom (Fab) equipment to the office, allowing engineers to remotely operate and monitor the machines directly.

::picture-and-text

### Multi‑Screen Strategic Monitoring
Supporting 4‑grid, 9‑grid, 16‑grid, and large strategic dashboard modes.

### Remote Operation and Management
Backend Management, Personnel Information Management, and Equipment Management 

### Log Recording (Log)
Login Log
Equipment Operation Log
System Configuration Change Log
Safety Button Operation Log

### Operation Recording
Supporting video recording for remote equipment operation, enabling efficient traceability and auditing.
