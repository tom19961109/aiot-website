## Manufacturing Execution System

With Lot Control as the core, the system connects work orders, WIP, exceptions, warehousing, packaging, and traceability reports to establish a manufacturing execution system that manages the entire flow from production input to finished‑goods output.

::picture-and-text

### Core Features

#### Standardized Manufacturing Process

Starting from foundational data such as machines, processes, routes, and products, the manufacturing process is first standardized before moving into on‑site execution.

#### Full WIP Visibility

Driven by lot status, every on‑site operation is executed with clear contextual traceability, enabling managers to grasp the real‑time situation on the shop floor.

#### Comprehensive Traceability Capability

Extending from work‑order analysis to full Lot history, the system enables quality, process, and management teams to quickly pinpoint issues.

### Designed for Semiconductor and Precision Manufacturing

In semiconductor and precision‑manufacturing environments—characterized by multi‑site operations, complex process flows, and high traceability requirements—an MES must balance process flexibility with practical shop‑floor needs.

With proven experience implementing customized MES solutions for semiconductor and precision‑manufacturing clients, we design and integrate systems based on each factory’s unique processes, equipment, and management model, ensuring the solution truly aligns with real shop‑floor operations.

### Implementing an MES does not necessarily require starting with a large and complex system architecture.

Starting with the most fundamental and core architecture, the MES supports seamless shop‑floor integration, enabling enterprises to adopt the system with a lower entry barrier and achieve rapid deployment. Additional solutions can then be progressively integrated according to the factory’s automation and smart‑manufacturing needs.
