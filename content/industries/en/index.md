## Packaging and Testing

For the diverse and complex equipment and processes in OSAT factories, we provide equipment integration and RPA‑based process automation services that eliminate data silos between testers, probers, and sorters. Through SPC analysis of test data, the system enables rapid understanding of product performance and establishes complete traceability from wafer to finished goods, enhancing overall production efficiency and customer trust.

## PCB & Passive Components

We help PCB and passive component manufacturers implement smart manufacturing by integrating key equipment such as AOI and electrical testers through EAP, enabling automated production workflows. With SPC systems controlling process parameters, consistent quality is ensured. Our traceability system tracks the full production history of each lot, allowing rapid identification, analysis, and corrective action when quality issues arise.

## Display Panel

Display panel manufacturing generates massive volumes of data, and our software solution serves as the core of your data management. Through EAP, we integrate equipment across the Array, Cell, and Module stages, while SPC monitors key indicators such as Mura, luminance, and chromaticity. Combined with RPA‑based automated report generation and a full traceability system, our solution helps you uncover quality trends from large datasets and optimize production decisions.
